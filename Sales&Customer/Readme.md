# Sales & Customer Revenue Management Growth – Power BI Project
## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance, revenue growth, and customer segmentation for a business using Power BI. The goal is to create interactive dashboards that provide actionable insights into revenue trends, product sales, and account segmentation to help drive strategic business decisions.

## 🗂 Dataset Description

* Dataset Name: Sales & Customer Revenue Dataset

* Format: CSV / Excel

* Columns include (examples):

* Order_ID – Unique identifier for each order

* Customer_ID – Unique identifier for each customer

* Product – Product name or category

* Revenue – Revenue generated from each sale

* Quantity_Sold – Number of products sold

* Sales_Date – Date of sale

* Region – Customer or sales region

* Account_Type – Customer segment (e.g., Gold, Silver, Bronze)

## 📊 Dashboards Overview

The project contains three interactive dashboards within a single Power BI project:

### 1. Revenue Dashboard

Focuses on overall revenue growth and trends.

Insights include:

Revenue trends over time (monthly, quarterly, yearly).

Contribution of different regions and sales channels to total revenue.

Charts used: Line charts, Area charts, KPI cards, Column charts.

### 2. Sales & Product Dashboard

Analyzes product-wise sales and performance.

Insights include:

Top-selling products and categories.

Quantity sold and revenue generated per product.

Seasonal or monthly product demand trends.

Charts used: Bar charts, Pie charts, Stacked column charts, Tree maps.

### 3. Account Segmentation Dashboard

Explores customer segmentation and behavior.

Insights include:

Revenue contribution per customer segment (Gold, Silver, Bronze).

Customer retention and growth trends.

Segment-wise purchase patterns and lifetime value.

Charts used: Clustered bar charts, Heat maps, Donut charts, KPI cards.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

* Power BI Desktop – Data modeling, transformations, and visualization

* Data Transformation: Power Query for cleaning and preprocessing

* DAX: Calculated measures for revenue growth, segment KPIs, and trends

* Visualizations: Line charts, Bar charts, Tree maps, Pie/Donut charts, KPI cards, Heat maps
